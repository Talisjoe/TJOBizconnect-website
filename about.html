About TJO BizConnect

Learn more about our purpose and how TJO BizConnect connects people with local businesses.

Who We Are

TJO BizConnect is a simple online directory designed to help people find businesses and service providers in Papua New Guinea.

The website brings useful business information together in one central location, making it easier for users to discover local businesses and services.

How We Started

TJO BizConnect was developed to address the difficulty of finding reliable information about local businesses when information is scattered across different platforms.

Our Brand Promises

Easy to Use

We aim to make business information easy to find and understand.

Local Focus

Our focus is on connecting users with businesses and services in Papua New Guinea.

Accessible Information

We aim to provide useful business information in one convenient location.

Our Process

We collect basic business information, organise it into categories, and present it through a simple website so users can easily browse local businesses and services.
